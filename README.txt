ECONOMY SIM – PHILIPPINE BUSINESS CHALLENGE
============================================

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Group-based business simulation with five economic events
- Interactive five-question formative quiz with explanations
- Illustrative economic data visualizer and team reflection
- Teacher dashboard with local records and CSV export
- PWA manifest and service worker for installation/offline caching

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Upload the contents of this folder to a static web host that serves HTTPS.
   Examples include GitHub Pages, Netlify, or a school web server.
3. Open the deployed HTTPS address in Chrome, Edge, or another supported browser.
4. On supported devices, use the browser's Install app / Add to Home Screen option.
5. Open the app once while online so its files can be cached for later offline use.

IMPORTANT CLASSROOM LIMITATIONS
- This starter version stores records in the browser's local storage on the device being used.
- It does not synchronize team records from student phones to the teacher's device.
- For a true multi-device teacher dashboard, add a backend/database and team login or room code.
- The economic chart and simulation effects are fictional teaching examples, not official current Philippine statistics or forecasts.
- The app does not collect student personal information. Use team names rather than full student names.

SUGGESTED 50–60 MINUTE USE
1. Form teams and assign roles: decision-maker, recorder, analyst, reporter.
2. Give each team one device and let them complete the five business events.
3. Have teams take the five-question knowledge check.
4. Examine the illustrative cost trend and write a short interpretation.
5. Use the Teacher Desk to review local records, export CSV, and lead a debrief.

LEARNING TARGET
Explain how inflation, income and employment, technology, operating costs, competition,
and consumer behavior affect business decisions and industry development.
